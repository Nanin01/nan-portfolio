# 💰 P/R Control

## Overview

A budget control and reporting system developed to monitor budget information by quarter and fiscal year.

## Business Problem

Budget information needed to be clearly presented to support monitoring of allocated, used, and remaining budgets.

## Solution

Developed a system that provides clear budget information by Budget No., quarter, and fiscal year.

## Key Features

* Budget No. management
* Quarterly budget reporting
* Fiscal year reporting
* Total budget monitoring
* Used budget monitoring
* Remaining budget monitoring
* Budget reports
