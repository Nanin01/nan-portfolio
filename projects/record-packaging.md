# 📦 Record Packaging

## Overview

A web-based dashboard system developed to replace paper-based daily packaging records.

## Business Problem

Packaging activities were recorded using paper documents, resulting in manual data entry, paper usage, and limited visibility of current production status.

## Solution

Developed digital packaging records and dashboards to provide real-time visibility of packaging activities by production line.

## Key Features

* Daily packaging records
* Production line task lists
* Real-time packaging status
* Dashboard reporting
* Digital record management
* Production monitoring
