# 💻 IT Bring Out Form

## Overview

An online system developed to control and manage computer equipment taken outside the company.

## Business Problem

The previous process required multiple paper forms and could take up to one hour per transaction.

## Solution

Developed an online approval and tracking system to digitize the computer equipment bring-out process.

## Key Features

* Equipment request
* Online approval
* Equipment information
* Request status tracking
* Digital documentation
* Approval workflow

## My Role

* Process analysis
* System design
* Frontend development

* Workflow implementation
* Testing and maintenance

## Business Impact

* 📄 Reduced approximately **816 sheets of paper per year**
* ⏱️ Saved approximately **816 working hours per year**
* 💰 Reduced costs by approximately **THB 79,152 per year**
* Reduced approval time
* Improved operational convenience
* Replaced a paper-based process
