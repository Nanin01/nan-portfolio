# 📊 Check Stock FG

## Overview

A web-based system developed to support monthly and yearly finished goods stock counting.

## Business Problem

Manual stock counting and recording could result in counting and data entry errors and made inventory information more difficult to manage.

## Solution

Developed a web-based stock counting system to improve inventory accuracy and provide better visibility of finished goods stock information.

## Key Features

* Monthly stock counting
* Yearly stock counting
* Finished goods inventory management
* Stock data recording
* Data validation
* Inventory reporting
