# ⭐ Customer Satisfaction Survey

## Overview

A web-based customer satisfaction survey system developed to digitize customer feedback collection and management reporting.

## Business Problem

Sales staff previously distributed surveys manually through email, which required additional coordination and could delay data collection and reporting.

## Solution

Developed a web-based survey system that allows customers to submit feedback directly online while providing management with real-time reporting.

## Key Features

* Online customer survey
* Customer feedback collection
* Web-based submission
* Survey data management
* Real-time dashboard
* Management reporting
