# ♻️ Scrap Management System

## Overview

A web-based scrap management system developed to replace paper-based and multi-step manual processes for the Accounting Department.

## Business Problem

The previous process involved paper documents, repetitive data entry, and multiple manual steps, resulting in additional workload and a higher risk of data entry errors.

## Solution

Developed a centralized web-based system to digitize the scrap management process and reduce redundant data entry.

## Key Features

* Scrap information management
* Digital workflow
* Data validation
* Centralized data management
* Reporting
* Process tracking
