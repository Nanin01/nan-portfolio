# 🧾 E-Billing System

## Overview

An online billing system developed to streamline invoice submission and improve billing status tracking.

## Business Problem

Vendors previously needed to submit invoices on-site and relied on email communication to follow up on billing status.

## Solution

Developing an online billing platform that allows vendors to submit invoices and track billing status through the web.

## Key Features

* Online invoice submission
* Billing status tracking
* Vendor access
* Invoice information management
* Billing workflow
* Web-based status monitoring

## Business Impact

* Reduced vendor travel time for invoice submission
* Reduced missed invoices
* Reduced dependency on email communication
* Improved billing process transparency
* Improved processing speed and accuracy
