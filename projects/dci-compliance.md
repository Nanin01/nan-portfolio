# 📚 DCI Compliance

## Overview

A web-based compliance document portal developed to provide centralized access to compliance-related documents.

## Business Problem

Employees needed convenient access to updated compliance information, while document distribution and management required significant manual coordination.

## Solution

Developed a centralized web portal for employees to access up-to-date compliance documents.

## Key Features

* Centralized document access
* Document management
* Compliance information
* Document status tracking
* Search and access
* Web-based document portal
