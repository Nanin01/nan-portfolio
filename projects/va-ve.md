# 💡 Value Analysis & Value Engineering (VA/VE)

## Overview

A web-based system developed to support Value Analysis and Value Engineering (VA/VE) projects for part and material cost reduction.

## Business Problem

VA/VE activities required effective tracking of projects, cost analysis, and cost reduction results.

## Solution

Developed a centralized web-based system to support VA/VE workflows and provide better visibility of cost reduction projects.

## Key Features

* VA/VE project management
* Part and material cost tracking
* Cost analysis
* Cost reduction tracking
* Project status monitoring
* Reporting
* Paperless workflow
